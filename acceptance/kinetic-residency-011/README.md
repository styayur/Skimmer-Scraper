# Kinetic City collision identity after live eviction

All 48 equal-revision comparisons across opposite visitation orders matched the chunk identity, fingerprint and collision bytes. Stable reproduced its original collision SHA-256 in 24 of 24 reloads. Ephemeral advanced exactly one retained revision and changed the collision SHA-256 in 24 of 24 reloads. All 48 reloads issued a new socket lease and created a new collision geometry instance. The socket ID, absolute position and logical contact surface survived every reload. [Six raw receipts](results/) · [Hosted run](https://github.com/styayur/Skimmer-Scraper/actions/runs/46123403397) · [Original issue 70](https://github.com/styayur/Skimmer-Scraper/issues/70)

## Recorded routes

Each seed and mode used two fresh browser contexts. Both first visited cells `(-10,-7)`, `(11,-8)`, `(9,10)` and `(-12,9)` in the same order to establish comparable retained revision histories. The first measured tour visited them in that order. The other context visited them in reverse. Both then reversed their own routes for the reload tour. Every visit ended at parking cell `(96,-96)`, outside all four target neighborhoods. The render origin moved from `(0,0,0)` to `(20000,0,-20000)` metres on each parking leg. All contexts reached the same starting revisions without extra alignment visits.

The test used the shipping title-screen pose control and waited for collision coverage and settled queues at each destination. V8's inspector exposed the active WorldStreamer already used by the flight query. The observer traced each chunk to its registered BVH, checked that the BVH owned the generated collision geometry, and resolved a real glide contact on that geometry near the owned north socket. It hashed the exact typed-array views of the BVH's positions followed by its indices. The receipts also contain separate SHA-256 values for positions and indices, their types, byte lengths and triangle counts. [Observer](observe.mjs) · [Tours, assertions and failure diagnostics](run-matrix.mjs)

At parking, the old chunk was absent from the streamer's managed map and collision index. Its semantic socket and lease disappeared, and the retirement and geometry-disposal counts increased. On reload the observer recorded the new geometry UUID and activation lease. Per cell in the listed order, Ephemeral revisions advanced `2→3`, `4→5`, `5→6` and `6→7` on all three seeds. Each transition matched the history entry recorded immediately after the earlier eviction. Stable stayed at revision zero in both directions.
