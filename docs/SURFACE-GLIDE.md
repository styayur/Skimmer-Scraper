# Surface Glide

Press Space or the left bumper during flight to arm glide. The controller setting −1 selects LB automatically. Standard pads use button 4. The unlabelled 2563:0526 mapping reports its left bumper at raw button 6. Setting an explicit button overrides detection. The menu, push-to-talk and boost bindings take precedence when they share a button.

Approach within 11 m of physical collision geometry with a tangential component to your velocity. The aircraft aims for a 6 m clearance. Pitch adjusts clearance and bank or yaw steers within the tangent plane. Boost continues to control speed. Press the same control to detach. Loss of a surface or an abrupt normal change returns the aircraft to flight. Arm again to attach elsewhere.

At each fixed tick the query returns the nearest pilot-facing triangle normal. On a rigid moving mesh the contact velocity comes from the current and previous transforms of the same mesh-local point. The controller projects relative velocity into the tangent plane, applies bounded normal acceleration and adds surface motion. The aircraft rotates toward the tangent frame with a rate-limited quaternion. The ordinary shield and swept collision rules still apply.

Actual attachment marks that run as Surface Glide Assisted. Disarming, pausing and detaching preserve the classification until the next run. Archived unassisted personal bests remain available. Decorative objects, distant terrain, the sky mirror and the animated dragon are excluded from the glide query.
